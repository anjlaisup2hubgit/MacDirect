Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

iMCQ is an unofficial student project. It is not run by Macquarie University. This page says what we keep, why, and how you control it.

What we store if you log in

We store your email and your password. The password is kept in a scrambled form that we cannot read. We store your display name. We store the societies you follow and your event plans, including any photo you add. If you lead a society, we also store your request and its approval. When you sign up, we record that you agreed to this.

Why we store it

We use it to let you log in and to keep your calendar. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for adverts.

What leaders make public

Once a leader is approved, their display name is shown on the "Who runs each society" page. Events and photos that leaders post for a society are public.

What we do not store

We do not store your location. If you press Directions to a drinking fountain or Tap It tower, your browser asks to share where you are. We use it only to work out that walk, and we do not keep it. A guest has no account. Guest browsing keeps only a few settings in your own browser, such as your language.

Messages you send us

If you use "Contact the developers", we store your message. We store your reply email only if you type one. Guests can send a message too. Please do not put private details in a message.

Other services that see your visit

Your data is kept with Supabase, a hosting service. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Map code loads from unpkg and jsDelivr. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap. These services can see your IP address when your browser asks them for files. Links to Google Maps and the Macquarie website open in a new tab and follow their own policies.

In your browser

Your browser keeps a few small settings so the app works the way you left it. These are your language, calendar view, week start, lit path choice and guest choice. Your login session is also kept here.

Your choices

In Settings, under Data, you can download your data. You can also delete your account. Deleting removes your login, follows and event plans. Events that a leader posted stay, but they no longer show who posted them. Messages you sent us stay, but your reply email is removed.

Questions

Use "Contact the developers" in Settings and choose "A privacy concern". We may update this page. The date at the top will change when we do.
